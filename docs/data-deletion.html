Claimit – Data Deletion Instructions

If you used Facebook Login to sign in to the Claimit app and want to delete all data associated with your account, you can do so in one of the following ways:

Option 1 – Delete from the app

  1. Open the Claimit app.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm deletion. All your personal data (profile, bill history, rewards, and any Facebook-linked information) will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Option 2 – Email request

Send an email to support@claimit.in with the subject "Delete My Data". Include the email or phone number linked to your account. We will process the request and confirm deletion within 30 days.
