Privacy Policy – Claimit

Last updated: June 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Claimit:

  • Name, email address, and phone number (provided during registration or via Facebook Login)
  • Location data (area/locality you select within the app)
  • Bill scan data (shop name, bill amount, date) for reward calculations
  • Profile picture (if you choose to upload one)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide loyalty rewards, cashback, and deal discovery features
  • To send notifications about offers and rewards
  • To improve app performance and user experience

3. Facebook Login

If you sign in using Facebook, we receive your public profile and email address from Facebook. We do not store your Facebook password. You can remove Claimit's access from your Facebook account settings at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share anonymised, aggregated data with advertisers. Individual user data is never shared with third parties without your consent.

5. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at support@claimit.in.
